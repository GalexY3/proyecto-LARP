## Scientific identity
Target: distributed retrieval of **five** spatially varying latent modalities using one scalar observation per sensor and directed 6-neighbor graph. The deterministic estimator finds the nearest *reachable* same-type observation at hop 1 or 2 and combines modalities with known weights; missing values are imputed as zero, with no retraining. 400 sensors, exact avian type counts (163,85,68,50,34), fixed 3 geometry seeds [1103,1109,1117], 8 field seeds [12013,12037,12041,12049,12073,12097,12101,12109]. Unit = synthetic independently generated field realization conditioned on a geometry, NOT independent physical observation. Correlated repeated node predictions are aggregated into a single NMSE per seed-field.
**Explicit limitations:** `mhu_knn` uses a finite **proxy spectral optimizer**, not the original Scale3D native avian MHU, and cannot certify asymptotic HU. `cvt_knn` is a type-conditioned Lloyd CVT proxy, not original LOCAL; `expander_deg6` is a random six-shift regular circulant under relabeling, **not a certified expander with spectral gap**. No trained learned MoE, no actual visual or human observations. Synthetic priors are imposed, hence measured functional effects remain within the generator, not external independent evidence.
## Fixed nine rival graph constructions (exact directed out-degree 6, equal 400 sensors)
`grid_knn` (20×20 grid, 6-nearest), `cvt_knn` (Lloyd per type, six-nearest), `mhu_knn` (spectral proxy, six-nearest), `random_deg6` (random fixed outdegree), `expander_deg6` (randomly relabeled circulant), `grid_type`, `cvt_type`, `mhu_type` (four nearest **other modality** reps plus two nearest extra), `mhu_hybrid` (two nearest plus fill missing modalities and remainder). Similar graph degree, exact exposure to own scalar signal. Different graph design cost, different in-degree/edge lengths, coordinate geometry, and training (no learning): not a hardware/computation FLOP parity or an isolated causal MHU contrast.

No actual source field is withheld from simulator generating test labels, but estimator uses only observed values and declared weights. No test-based choice of a best method.
