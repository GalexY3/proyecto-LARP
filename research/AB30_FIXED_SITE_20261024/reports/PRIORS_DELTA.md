# AB30 fixed-site — cumulative delta priors (append-only)
- **FS-P01:** finite-type spectral power can be directly improved by recoloring fixed sensor sites; not evidence of asymptotic MHU or functional superiority. Q01–Q06; strong prior art SIGGRAPH 2010.
- **FS-P02:** at identical sites/quotas, spectral labeling can improve modality coverage over random labeling, but greedy farthest-first beats it in 4/6 held-out modality conditions. Q07–Q12.
- **FS-P03:** local inverse-distance reconstruction results depend on modality count and Fourier mode; held-out strong control wins in 3/6 tasks. Q13–Q18.
- **FS-P04:** Fourier ridge estimator changes ranking. Last field (3,0) outside fixed basis. Q19–Q24, 2/6 G strong-control favorable.
- **FS-P05:** OOD synthetic Fourier fields and IID global sensor sites reduce transfer; Q25–Q30 strong-control favorable only 2/6.
- **FS-P06 (1/3 only, LARP-1-005):** method audit: matched positions isolate modality allocation from site geometry; finite low-k criterion does NOT guarantee superior task-level performance against farthest-first. Known theory and prior art, not 2/3.
- **FS-P07:** Public-image audit conducted AFTER 30 cycles, six images; IDW union20 spectral beats farthest-first 3/6, not external confirmatory validation.
