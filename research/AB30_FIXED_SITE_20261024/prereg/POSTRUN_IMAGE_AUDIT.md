# POST-SELECTION ADVERSARIAL REAL-IMAGE AUDIT (not cycle 31)
Date 2026-10-24, after all C01-C30 E/G and signs were observed. **Explicitly exploratory**, cannot be external confirmatory replication.
Six preselected skimage bundled photographs/scans: camera, coins, moon, page, horse, text. All are *real images*, not new field observations, may have previous public exposure; image boundary is not periodic and using torus-based IDW on edges is a modeling limitation held identical among arms.
Exactly same frozen sensor coordinates and modality quotas, fixed-site spectral labels, random labels, and greedy farthest label assignment per seed. Use 8 G seeds [201..208] and evaluate BOTH (i) rare union of types3+4 n20 and (ii) rarest type4 n8. Normalize each image to [0,1], sample points using bilinear map_coordinates(mode='wrap') and evaluate on 30x30 torus GRID; estimator is periodic four-neighbor inverse-distance weighting (IDW), plus Fourier ridge of original source as second control. No task training, no hyperparam tuning, all images and both masks reported. Endpoint image MSE A(spectral)-C(farthest), plus A-B(random) secondary. **Prediction H1:** A beats farthest in majority of 6 observed images for union20 using IDW; H0 not. Falsifier: ≤3/6 favor A, or severe model-dependent reversals. Multiplicity and correlated pixels/images preclude confirmation.
Novelty prior art already exists: SIGGRAPH 2010 Multi-Class Blue Noise Sampling, 2013 Blue Noise Controlled Aliasing and retina MHU 2014. New 1/3 only if nonduplicative and meaningful after strong controls; no 2/3 from this post-hoc source switch.
